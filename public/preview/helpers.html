Community Helpers, three ways to make it hands-on

Rough mock-ups you can try with a mouse or finger. The same facts as today (who helps, what they use, where they work, how a town runs), answered by doing.

A. Who do you call?

A little town. Trouble pops up at the house ("Smoke is coming from the kitchen!"). Tap the right building and the helper's vehicle drives over. Pre-K hears it read aloud and sees pictures only.

B. Pack the kit

A helper is off to work with an empty bag. Drag the two things they need from the shelf into the bag. Wrong things bounce back with a word about who really uses them.

C. Run the town (Grades 4 to 8)

The town's tax chest. Drag a coin onto each thing taxes should pay for. Private things (the sweet shop, a birthday party) send the coin back. Later rounds: vote for the mayor, pass a law in parliament.
