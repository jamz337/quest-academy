The New Skill page, four ways to make it hands-on

Today the page shows a worked example as text cards revealed with "Next step", then one multiple-choice try. These mock-ups put the child's hands on it. Try them with a mouse or finger.

A. Watch the hand, then you

The lesson happens on the game's own board. A ghost hand does the first one while the villager explains, then the child does one on the same board. If they wait, the right spot starts to glow.

New skill: animal homes
🧑‍🌾

B. Do each step yourself

Each step is something the child does, not reads. "Tap 3 apples", "Now tap 2 more", "How many altogether?" The next step only comes when this one is done.

New skill: adding

C. Spot the mistake (Grades 3 to 8)

Buddy has worked one out but slipped up. The child taps the wrong step, then picks what it should say. Finding a mistake shows they understand every step, not just the answer.

New skill: adding with carrying
47 + 38 = ?

I worked this one out. One of my steps is wrong. Can you find it?

D. Your turn with a hint ladder

Instead of one fixed tip, the child climbs hints only when they want one: a picture, then the rule, then half the working. Fewer hints earn more stars, so it is their choice.
