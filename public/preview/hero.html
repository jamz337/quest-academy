Live recolouring of the current drawn heroes (front, standing). The real hero sheets will use marker colours, so hair and every piece of clothing swap cleanly; here the boy's black hair stays black.

A. One drawing, recoloured

One hero sheet per hairstyle. Skin, hair, top, bottom and shoes are colour swaps the child picks. Fewest drawings, and the look stays consistent.

B. Pick a hero from a gallery

Twelve to sixteen complete heroes drawn once each, no editing. Simplest to build and always looks right, but two children often end up with the same hero.

C. Layered parts

Body, hair and outfit drawn as separate layers that stack. The most combinations, but every layer must be drawn to fit every pose, which image generators get wrong often.
