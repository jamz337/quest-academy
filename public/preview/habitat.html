Habitat Match, three ways to make it hands-on

Rough mock-ups you can try with a mouse or finger. The same animals, homes, adaptations and food chains as today, answered by doing.

A. Rescue and release

Ranger Rio's truck has rescued animals. Drag each one into its home. It settles in and moves about (the fish swims, the monkey swings). A wrong home sends it back with a reason.

B. Help it survive

An animal is moving to a new home. Drag the two body parts or habits it needs there onto it. The thermometer and the weather show why: thick fur on the ice, a hump in the desert.

C. Build the food chain (Grades 4 to 8)

Drag the living things into the arrows, from the sun to the top hunter. When it is right, energy flows along the chain. Harder rounds add a decomposer at the end.
