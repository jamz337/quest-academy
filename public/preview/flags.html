Flag Finder, three ways to make it hands-on

Rough mock-ups you can try with a mouse or finger. The flags are the game's own drawings. Same facts as today (which flag, whose flag, where in the world), answered by doing.

A. Paint the flag

A blank flag with its shapes drawn in. Pick a paint pot, tap a part to fill it, then Check. Wrong parts wobble and say what colour goes there on the second try.

B. Fly it where it belongs

A map of the world. Drag the flag to the part of the world its country is in. Older grades drop it on the country's spot and name the capital.

C. Flag pairs

Cards face down. Turn two at a time to match each flag with its country's name. Pre-K would match a flag with the same flag.
