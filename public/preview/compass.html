Compass Quest, three ways to make it hands-on

Rough mock-ups you can try with a mouse or finger. Same questions as today, but answered by doing rather than by picking a button.

A. Sail the ship

The map is a grid. Orders come in ("Sail 2 north, then 3 east"); tap the compass to move the ship and reach the treasure. Wrong way and you hit rocks.

B. Turn the needle

Drag the needle round the rose to point where the question says: south-west, or 90°, or towards the sunrise. Older grades get degrees.

C. Read the harbour map

A little map of History Harbor. "The lighthouse is which way from the fort?" Tap the place, or tap the direction word, and the answer walks there.
